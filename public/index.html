01Verification · the three gates
Article IStage one

Original score report

We confirm the official score document for the exact examination the tutor intends to teach — the document itself, issued by the board.

  • IB final transcript
  • AP Score Report
  • A-Level Statement of Results
  • College Board official report
Passes only on confirmation
Article IIStage two

Enrolment certificate

We confirm a current enrolment or graduation certificate from Seoul National, Korea University or Yonsei. Every tutor on the registry comes from the SKY overseas-Korean and international community.

  • Certificate of enrolment
  • Certificate of graduation
Passes only on confirmation
Article IIIStage three

One-to-one interview

A score does not guarantee the ability to teach. The founding team runs a mock lesson and interview in person, and only those who demonstrate genuine explanatory power and responsibility pass.

  • Mock lesson
  • Subject interview
The pass rate is kept low

※ The red seal is applied only to tutors who have cleared all three stages.

04Our story

Seonbae began in a shared university dormitory room. Three people from overseas-Korean and international-school backgrounds — who had sat IB and SAT themselves and gone on to Seoul National, Korea University and Yonsei — decided to build what they had wanted most as students: the chance to learn from a verified senior who had already walked the same road.

B1Story image placeholder

International curricula are difficult for a conventional Korean academy to handle. The SAT, AP and TOEFL have all moved to digital formats in recent years, and the generation that lived through that change teaches it most accurately. Every tutor at Seonbae belongs to that generation.

B2Story image placeholder

We start in Seoul, but lessons open online from anywhere in the world. The Korean communities in Hong Kong and the United States are next.

Two birds, one stone. Tutors earn fairly and students learn properly — inside one network that can be trusted.
