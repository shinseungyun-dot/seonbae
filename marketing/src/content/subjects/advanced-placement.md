Taking several AP classes at once can be demanding. Our tutors carry that burden with you, and make it their priority to help you move ahead.

Explore new concepts before the rest of the class reaches them. Find weak spots in your knowledge before it is too late. Every lesson prepares you for the exam and moves you closer to a 5. You will learn that answers that read well are not always the answers that earn marks.

As exam season approaches, tutors prepare you for the exam hall. Time limits and gaps in knowledge become familiar problems with clear solutions.
